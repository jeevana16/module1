Zepto Refund Policy

Refund eligibility depends on the reason for the refund, the order status, the payment method, and the applicable refund rules.

When a refund is approved, the refund is processed through the applicable payment provider.

The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may vary depending on the payment provider or bank.

Customers should contact Zepto support with their order details if they have questions about an expected refund.
