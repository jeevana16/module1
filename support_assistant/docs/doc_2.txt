Zepto Order Cancellation Policy

Order cancellation depends on the current status of the order and the applicable cancellation rules.

A customer may be able to cancel an order before certain fulfillment stages are completed.

Once an order has progressed to a later fulfillment stage, cancellation may no longer be available.

Customers should check the order status in the Zepto application. If cancellation is not available, they can contact Zepto support for assistance.
