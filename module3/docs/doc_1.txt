Zepto Delivery Policy

Delivery time estimates depend on several factors, including the customer's delivery location, store availability, product availability, traffic conditions, weather, and operational conditions.

The estimated delivery time shown to the customer is an estimate and may change because of unexpected delays or operational issues.

Customers should check the delivery status and estimated arrival time in the Zepto application for the latest information.

If an order is delayed, the customer can contact Zepto support and provide the order details so the support team can check the current status.
